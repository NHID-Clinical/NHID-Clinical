# Discovery interview script: automated callers on payer lines

**Purpose:** learn how payer teams experience automated callers today, before proposing
anything. Target: 15 conversations with payer operations, contact-center,
provider-services, privacy or security leaders.

**Rules for the interviewer**

- Ask about what they did, not what they would do. Past behaviour beats opinions.
- Do not pitch until the last five minutes, and only if they ask.
- Do not describe NHID-Clinical as deployed, adopted, validated or compliant. It is in the
  Research stage ([claim-boundaries.md](claim-boundaries.md#stage)).
- Never ask for or accept member data, recordings or transcripts on the call.
- Take notes in the template at the end, within an hour of the call.

---

## 1. Screening (2 minutes)

1. What is your role, and which lines or queues does it cover?
2. Roughly how many provider-initiated calls do those lines take in a week?

## 2. What happens today (10 minutes)

3. Tell me about the last time a representative realised the caller was automated. What
   happened next?
4. How do representatives currently tell an automated caller from a person?
5. What do representatives check before giving eligibility or claim status to a caller
   who says they are from a provider's office?
6. Has an automated caller ever received information it should not have? How did you
   find out?
7. Do you have a written policy for automated callers? Who owns it?

## 3. Measurement (5 minutes)

8. Do you capture call transcripts or turn-level logs today? Where do they live, and who
   can export them?
9. If you wanted to know how many calls last month were automated, how would you find
   out?

## 4. What would change the answer (8 minutes)

10. Under what conditions, if any, would you let an automated caller receive eligibility
    or claim status?
11. What evidence would you need to see first: about the agent, the provider it acts for,
    or this specific call?
12. Who would have to approve that decision? Who would block it?
13. What have you already tried or evaluated for this? What happened?

## 5. Pilot interest (5 minutes, only if relevant)

14. If you could measure, on your own machine, how often automated callers disclose
    themselves and whether data moves before they do, who would want to see the result?
15. What would stop you running something like that in the next quarter?
