## Participant instructions (read before consenting)

You are helping test software that checks how an automated phone "voice agent"
behaves on a mock healthcare administrative call. You will **act out** a short,
scripted call using a **made-up identity and made-up insurance details** — not
your own, and not any real person's.

- You will play a role (the calling "agent" or the "payer representative").
- The call is **recorded** so the software can be tested on it.
- **Use only the fictional details on your scenario card.** Do not say your own
  real name, real insurance, real medical information, or anyone else's.
- You can stop at any time, and you can ask us to delete your recording at any
  time, for any reason, with no consequence.
- You will not be calling any real company, clinic, insurer, or person.

## Consent statement (spoken on the recording, before testing begins)

> "This call is being recorded to test voice-agent governance software. I am a
> consenting adult volunteer. I will use only fictional identities and fictional
> insurance information, not real personal or health information about myself or
> anyone else. I understand the recording will be used to evaluate the software,
> stored securely, and deleted on a stated schedule, and that I may withdraw my
> consent and have my recording deleted at any time. I consent to being
> recorded."

Each participant says this (or affirms "I consent" after the observer reads it)
**before** any scenario recording starts. The consent segment is captured, then
kept separate from — and not published with — the scenario audio.

## What we collect and keep

- **Audio** of the acted call (fictional content only), stored access-controlled
  outside the code repository, deleted per the retention schedule in the
  protocol (suggested: within 30 days of transcription/labelling, sooner on
  withdrawal).
- **A transcript**, verified to contain no real identities, which may be kept as
  a synthetic-equivalent test fixture.
- **No** real names, real contact details beyond what's needed to honour a
  withdrawal request, and **no** PHI (there is none, because identities are
  fictional).

## Withdrawal

Tell the observer at any time. We delete your audio and any transcript derived
from it. There is no penalty and you need give no reason.

## What this is used for

To measure, as a research exercise, whether the software correctly flags
behaviours like an agent not disclosing it is automated, or protected
information being requested too early. Results are reported as tests on
**scripted, fictional** calls — never as evidence about real healthcare calls or
real deployments.
