How to contribute

What we're looking for

💬

Technical feedback

Implementation feasibility, edge cases in the proposed requirements, gaps in coverage, and interoperability concerns.

🏥

Real-world experience

Payer-side call handling patterns, AI caller behaviors you've encountered, and operational gaps this proposal doesn't yet address — including the NPI spoofing scenario where a valid public NPI is used to impersonate a provider on a voice call.

⚖️

Regulatory context

HIPAA officer feedback, payer operations input, and legal or regulatory analysis of this proposal's positioning and scope.
