# Editorial Review — Chapter 14: Pilot Programs

*Review pass following the first complete draft.*

## Overall assessment

The chapter discharges Chapter 5's registered debt (a worked pilot
narrative, labeled composite) and does so with the correct labeling —
the opening note declares the composite before the scenario begins,
and the examples section repeats it. The four-deaths structure is the
chapter's organizing achievement: each failure mode is real,
recognizable, and paired with a prevention that was already built into
the program structure, which makes the chapter read as design rather
than warning. "The stop that was a success" may be the book's most
quietly important example — it makes honest null findings career-safe,
which is a precondition for every honest pilot that follows.

## Critique

1. **The composite labeling is the book's most explicit yet — keep it
   as the template.** The opening italic note plus the examples-section
   reminder is the right double-lock. The consistency pass should
   retrofit this pattern to the earlier chapters whose composites were
   flagged (4, 7, 9, 10, 13).
2. **"Fourteen months later" gives the composite a specific outcome
   horizon.** Harmless as composite texture, but it implies a typical
   timeline the book has no basis to project. Generalize to "a year
   on." Queued — minor integrity class.
3. **The public-good argument ("pilot discipline is a public good")
   is new and slightly grand.** It is defensible — the framework's
   materials do seek partners and the evidence base is genuinely
   thin — but the sentence should stay tethered to that fact rather
   than to a civic register. Light edit queued.
4. **Phase four's handoff ("the pilot designation retires") is the
   right boundary with Chapters 15/17** — verified against both
   briefs; no overlap. Clean.

## Weak arguments identified

- **"There is nothing in it for anyone to veto"** overstates —
  legal can veto data handling, and unions or works councils exist in
  some shops. The observe-only design minimizes veto surface; it does
  not zero it. Soften queued.
- **The partner-pilot example asserts both-sides-same-evidence as
  collapsing adversarial framing** — the mechanism is sound and the
  configuration is genuinely what the shadow-partner language
  describes, but "sign without dispute" is composite tidiness again.
  Add friction beat, per Chapter 8's incident precedent. Queued.

## Transitions

- **In:** Part IV opens at organizational altitude as planned; the
  kickoff scenario immediately distinguishes this part's subject
  (organizations) from Part III's (systems). Verified.
- **Out:** hands Chapter 15 the standing-regime brief. Chapter 15's
  accumulated debts, now formally due: CAS composition, Goodhart
  treatment, threshold-hardening, observability/dashboard thread
  (three deferrals), and the latency-companion reporting discipline.
