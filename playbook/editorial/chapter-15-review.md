# Editorial Review — Chapter 15: Metrics

*Review pass following the first complete draft.*

## Overall assessment

The chapter clears the book's largest accumulated brief — CAS
composition (delivered, with the prices-evidence insight), the Goodhart
treatment (delivered as the four stations, the chapter's structural
contribution), threshold-hardening (station four, with the opening
scenario's deferral modeling it), the thrice-deferred observability
thread (landed in implementation guidance with alert-tier mapping),
and the tail-reporting discipline (now policy, not habit). The
instrument/target distinction gives the chapter a thesis rather than
an inventory, and the quarterly-review scenario is the right opening:
a regime shown working before it is specified.

## Critique

1. **The wording-game example is the chapter's best and needs its
   composite label locally.** The book-level convention (established
   Chapter 14) covers it via the examples-section note — verify the
   note survives layout, since this example will be quoted out of
   context more than any other in Part IV.
2. **The four stations are the author's synthesis** (the framework
   supplies tiers, thresholds, and stability criteria; the hardening
   path is this book's arrangement). Same attribution convention as
   Chapter 10's ladder — one sentence queued, and the front-matter
   convention (two prior action items) now has a third client.
   **Front-matter item confirmed as required.**
3. **"Forty minutes end to end" — composite precision.** Harmless
   texture, borderline; leave, but the copyedit sweep for invented
   precision should adjudicate all instances with one standard.
4. **The tamper-detection claim ("distorts companions in predictable
   ways") is demonstrated for two cases** (wording game, average
   masking) **but stated generally.** Acceptable as designed intent of
   the companion set; soften "predictable" to "detectable" for
   precision. Queued.

## Weak arguments identified

- **The claim that event-completeness weighting prevents
  log-less gaming** is sound for the score but not for reality — a
  vendor could log richly and behave poorly elsewhere. The chapter
  doesn't overclaim (the composite routes to review; it doesn't
  acquit), but one clause noting the composite is triage, not
  absolution, would close the reading. Queued.
- **The refute-rate save example implies mapping rollback is easy.**
  Version discipline makes it *possible*; "rolled back,
  re-baselined" compresses real work. Minor; add "over a sprint."
